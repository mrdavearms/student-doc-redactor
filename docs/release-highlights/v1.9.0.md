### ✨ Paste text straight in — no document required

Got a paragraph copied out of an email, a portal, or someone else's report, and you
just need the names out of it? You can now paste it directly into the app instead of
saving it to a file first.

**Step 1 now offers three choices:** one document, a whole folder, or **paste text**.
Paste isn't a separate mode — both black-boxing and de-identifying work on pasted text
exactly as they do on documents, with the same review screen and the same **Who's who?**
step.

**Nothing touches your disk unless you ask.** A paste writes no audit log, no key file
and no temporary file. You get the cleaned result on screen with a **Copy** button, and
can save it if you want to — as a redacted PDF, or a de-identified `.txt`.

**In de-identify mode the name key stays on screen too**, in its own collapsed box with
its own separate Copy button, so copying the safe text can't accidentally drag the key
along with it.

There's a warning past 20,000 characters, and anything over 50,000 is declined with a
pointer back to the document pathway — at that size a document does the job better.

*Everything else works exactly as before.*
